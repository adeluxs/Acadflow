# AcadSyntra User Guide

## Notifications and step-by-step help

Use **Help centre** or **FAQs** to search guides for registration, departmental/institutional adoption, affiliation, memberships, academic access and Research Studio. Expand a guide for steps and links to the actual tools.

The notification bell opens categorised academic updates with filters, read controls and links to the relevant activity. **Notification Settings** lets you choose email, in-app and browser push, categories, timezone and quiet hours, and remove individual subscribed devices. Select **Enable Notifications** in the invitation to request browser permission; **Not Now** postpones it. If permission was denied, change it in the browser's site settings. On supported iPhones/iPads, install the website to the Home Screen first. Browser/OS support determines whether a closed-page notification can appear.

Onboarding reminders start after 12 hours and stop when setup is complete. Optional inactivity reminders are off until you enable engagement reminders, and require unfinished academic work. You can opt out in settings or through an optional email's unsubscribe confirmation. Security and account verification messages remain separate. Super Admins can inspect recorded delivery and engagement metrics under **Notification Analytics**; a provider accepting a message is distinct from confirmed inbox/device delivery.

The platform is now named **AcadSyntra**. The new name and violet book/connection symbol appear on public pages, account screens, dashboards, notifications, emails and new exports. Your account, institution membership, coursework, research and payment history carry over. Existing authenticator-app labels and previously exported documents may retain their historical names; no keys or academic files are replaced as part of the name change. Installed PWA names/icons may require a browser refresh or app update before the device shows the new identity.

**Audience:** students, lecturers, platform members, department administrators, university administrators, super administrators, supervisors, group leaders, creators and support staff  
**Product snapshot:** 2026-08-20

AcadSyntra brings academic coursework, submissions, attendance, research, publishing, communities, AI assistance and university administration together in one platform. What you can see depends on your role, university/department/course membership, subscription/entitlement, and whether an administrator has enabled the feature.

## 1. Before you start

### Explore the public website

Use **Features** to search capabilities by topic and **How it works** for registration, course learning, research and publishing journeys. **FAQs** and **Help center** have searchable, expandable answers. Product previews are illustrations with fictional content; the tools available to you still depend on your role, institution and enabled modules. The mobile Menu opens above the page and can be closed with its close button or Escape.

Use **Contact us** for account help, institution enquiries or privacy/security questions. Complete the fields, acknowledge the Privacy Policy and keep the reference shown after submission. Do not include credentials, card details or confidential academic records. The form saves your enquiry in a restricted platform inbox; it does not sign you up for marketing or promise a response time. Super Administrators manage it under **Website enquiries**, with Open and Closed views. Closing a message retains it. Policy pages identify any operator-specific information still requiring confirmation.

### Supported account roles

AcadSyntra has six main account roles:

| Role key | Display meaning | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `super_admin` | Super Admin | Platform-wide administration |
| `university_admin` | University Admin | Manage one university and its faculties/users/settings |
| `department_admin` | Department Admin | Manage a department, courses, lecturers, students and reports |
| `lecturer` | Lecturer | Teach courses, manage assignments/materials/attendance, review work and supervise research |
| `student` | Student | Learn, join courses, submit work, attend classes and use academic/research tools |
| `member` | Platform Member | Participate in Knowledge Hub, research, communities, groups and public academic ecosystem features |

Some responsibilities are not separate account roles:

- **Group Leader** — a user who leads a collaboration group.
- **Supervisor** — usually a lecturer assigned to a research/project supervision role.
- **Creator/Author** — a user who publishes Knowledge Hub content.
- **Event Organizer/Challenge Organizer/Judge** — contextual permissions in those modules.

### Availability can differ

AcadSyntra administrators can place features in:

- **Enabled** — normal use;
- **Maintenance** — temporarily unavailable to normal users;
- **Disabled/Unreleased** — hidden or unavailable to normal users.

Administrators can preview restricted features for testing. Subscription plans can also limit some features.

## 2. Registration, login and onboarding

### Registration

If self-registration is enabled, choose **Register**, provide the required account information and complete email verification. Institutions may also create/invite accounts or import students.

### Login

Use your email/username and password. AcadSyntra applies login rate limits and may require email verification or two-factor authentication before full access.

### Forgot password

Use **Forgot password** on the login screen. Follow the reset link sent to your email.

### Email verification

If the account is not verified, AcadSyntra can show the verification screen and allow another verification email to be requested.

### Two-factor authentication

If 2FA is required/enabled, complete the challenge after login. You can manage 2FA and recovery codes from **Account Security**.

### Onboarding

Choose a student, lecturer/supervisor, researcher, independent professional, author/publisher or other academic path. Existing account-role permissions still apply. Progress saves after each step and resumes when you return. The last step can open a first Research Studio or Final-Year Project Studio wizard after completing your profile; creation remains your explicit choice. Completed users continue through normal login without repeating setup. Research discipline, level and interests help personalise the workspace. If you use two-factor authentication, complete that challenge before onboarding.

Before normal application routes are available, new accounts complete role-aware onboarding. Depending on your account, onboarding can collect institution, faculty/department, programme/academic level, research/topic interests, skills, notification preferences and other profile information. Some optional steps can be skipped.

After setup, open **Manage Onboarding** from your account menu or **Complete your profile / Manage onboarding** on My Profile. You can edit any saved section or complete a skipped step without restarting. The dashboard shows a dismissible suggestion. Progress reflects applicable profile fields; skipped optional sections never remove workspace access. Students see programme/level fields; lecturers can update position, specialisation and supervision interests. Preferences autosave after changes and also provide a Save changes button.

Use **Search and request membership** when available, or **Memberships and invitations**, to connect the same account to an adopted department or institution. Existing work stays with your account. Directory selection is self-declared and grants no organisational access; verification and approval rules still apply. Department and institution administrators see a separate setup checklist linking to their existing verification, invitation, structure and configuration tools. New membership requests remain subject to platform availability.

Required-setup reminders link to the section that is actually missing. Optional profile suggestions require engagement opt-in and personalised recommendations, are at least 14 days apart, and stop for completed sections. Dismissing the profile suggestion also stops these optional suggestions. Quiet hours and channel preferences apply. See [Editable onboarding](EDITABLE_ONBOARDING.md).

After completion, you are redirected to your role-specific dashboard.

## 11. Research Studio

Research Studio is AcadSyntra's focused workspace for planning, writing and managing a research project. It is designed to work for an independent student/researcher first; institution-specific controls appear only when an administrator enables **Research Institutional Expansion** and the user has an institution affiliation.

### Create a methodology-specific project

Choose **New research project** or **Final-Year Project Studio**. The setup wizard collects your level, discipline, working title, objectives, methodology, institution when relevant and reference style. Choose a methodology-based report, the common Nigerian five-chapter format or your administrator's template. Quantitative, qualitative, mixed, review, legal/humanities, engineering/software, clinical/laboratory, field and creative designs have appropriate planning guidance. Your existing projects keep their sections and data.

Open **Plan & library** to create recoverable proposal, topic/gap, instrument, sampling, consent/ethics, analysis-plan, manuscript and defence worksheets. They start with prompts for you to complete. Add private documents or upload your institution's PDF/DOCX/TXT guideline, where permitted by administrator upload settings. Compare the extracted text with the original before confirming headings. Existing sections and writing are retained. Configure font, size, spacing, margins, citation instructions and an optional institutional bibliography pattern using saved reference fields, then export PDF, HTML or DOCX. Update the contents field in your word processor and check institutional formatting before submission.

The rich editor saves automatically and shows whether the save succeeded. If another tab saved a newer version, your save pauses: copy your unsaved draft, reload and compare versions. Keep the tab open when offline. Open **Section version history** to restore an earlier version as a new snapshot. Locked sections require an authorised correction/amendment. Basic tables are supported.

The sample-size planner records assumptions for estimating one proportion under simple random sampling; it is not a universal power calculator. With Dataset Management enabled, upload an anonymised CSV to compute descriptive summaries and a completeness chart. The analysis tool retains summaries and a checksum, not raw CSV rows. Use **Qualitative coding & themes** to connect real excerpts with source locators, code definitions, themes and interpretive memos. Saved resources can be inserted into an unlocked section. Archive superseded resources without deleting their history.

Reporting-framework links lead to the complete official PRISMA, CONSORT/SPIRIT, STROBE, COREQ and SRQR guidance where relevant. Confirm applicability with a supervisor or journal. These tools never issue ethics approval, authenticate findings or replace human academic review. Advanced statistical modelling, meta-analysis, simulation and model training require appropriate external analytical tools; record their actual outputs as evidence in your project. See [the detailed research guide](RESEARCH_STUDIO.md) for limits and academic sources.

### Core workspace

The default Research Studio keeps the essentials visible:

- structured research sections and version history;
- comments/corrections;
- milestones and project tasks;
- Research Assistant;
- literature discovery and the Project Reference Library;
- validation/similarity tools where enabled;
- research export.

The **Project Control Center** is the operational view of the project. Milestones show major research stages, tasks turn the work into actionable items, and the summary cards show progress, outstanding work, saved references and the next milestone. Optional operational modules such as meetings, collaboration/contribution tracking, datasets, archive/amendment controls, SIWES and seminar workflows appear only when the administrator enables them.

### Independent vs institutional research

Independent projects do not require a university, department or supervisor. When **Research Institutional Expansion** is enabled, an affiliated user can explicitly mark a new project as institutional and select a department.

Supervision uses an invitation workflow rather than direct assignment:

```text
Create institutional project
→ invite eligible lecturer
→ invitation is Pending
→ lecturer accepts or declines
→ only an accepted invitation becomes the project supervisor
```

If institutional expansion is disabled, normal users do not see the institutional checkbox, department selector or supervisor invitation controls.

### Literature discovery and Project Reference Library

Open **Literature & references** from the research project. Enter a topic, research question or keywords, optionally filter by year and choose scholarly sources. AcadSyntra can search configured scholarly indexes including OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, CORE, DOAJ, PubMed and arXiv.

Search results are scholarly metadata/abstract results. You can compare multiple papers, select several results and choose **Save selected papers**, or save one paper at a time. Duplicate results are reduced using DOI or normalized title/year matching.

A saved paper appears in the **Project Library**. For every saved reference you can record:

- your summary;
- methodology;
- key findings;
- limitations;
- contradictions/comparisons;
- research gap/relevance;
- review keywords.

These notes reopen with the previously saved content so the Project Library becomes a working literature-review evidence base rather than a bookmark list.

**Open paper** follows the scholarly source or DOI link. AcadSyntra does not bypass publisher subscriptions/paywalls and does not claim to store the full paper unless a lawful file has separately been added through an authorized workflow.

### Research Assistant and sources

The Research Assistant can use authorized project context and research evidence/notes available to AcadSyntra. A search result or abstract is not the same as the full paper. Users should read the actual source when available, record evidence accurately, and avoid treating AI-generated explanations as a substitute for the publication.

### Researcher/student/member

Create or join authorized research, write sections, respond to corrections, manage literature and track milestones according to your project membership.

### Supervisor/lecturer

When institutional expansion is enabled, an eligible lecturer can receive and accept a supervision invitation. Once accepted, the supervisor can use the project review permissions provided by the research policy. Supervisor is a contextual lecturer responsibility, not a separate account type.

### Research Validator

The validator provides structured AI-assisted validation where enabled. Human academic review remains authoritative.

## 16. Grounded AI Companion

The Grounded AI Companion appears inside an eligible Knowledge Hub publication.

Its purpose is **“ask this publication”**, not general web search.

Typical pipeline:

```text
your question
→ check that the question is meaningful
→ search the current publication's indexed content
→ check evidence relevance
→ use the configured AcadSyntra AI provider/rules
→ verify source citations
→ answer from the publication or say evidence is insufficient
```

Examples of good questions:

- “What are the main findings?”
- “Explain the methodology in simple terms.”
- “What limitations does the author identify?”
- “Which source section supports this conclusion?”

Meaningless input such as random keyboard strings should be rejected rather than sent to an expensive provider.

The companion does **not** automatically search the live open web. Using OpenAI/Gemini/Claude as the reasoning provider is different from web search; the publication remains the grounding source.

## 17. Main AI Assistant

The main AI Assistant is one interface containing specialized tools.

### Ask / Explain

- Student account → `study_assistant`
- Lecturer account → `lecturer_assistant`

### Improve Writing

Uses `writing_assistant`.

### Citation Review

Uses `citation_assistant`.

Each feature is routed through the same central AI settings. An administrator can configure the global provider/model or an intentional feature-specific override.

### My Study Space and Today

Students can choose **My Study Space** in the sidebar or **Study this material** on a course resource. Read a source, select a confusing passage, request a simple explanation and save it automatically. Then generate short practice from the same source, answer it and review the feedback. A saved quiz can be retaken without another AI generation request; new explanations/quizzes use your existing plan allowance.

You can also paste private notes or import readable PDF, Word and TXT documents where your administrator permits those file types. Notes belong to you and are linked to an enrolled course. Uploads are limited to 5 MB and 20,000 readable characters; scanned images need text extraction first.

The dashboard’s **Today** panel prioritizes an assignment closing soon, due revision or saved work. Missed answers return after one day; successful recall after a gap increases the revision interval, up to 30 days. Feedback shows expected answers and explanations after an attempt. Short written answers use exact wording checks. These practice results do not change official grades or predict exam performance. No extra email/push reminders are enabled automatically.

See [My Study Space](STUDENT_STUDY_SPACE.md) for the complete workflow and current limits.

### Teaching Studio for lecturers

Lecturers see **Teaching Studio** instead of student study tools. It brings together the next useful teaching actions: submissions waiting for feedback, open student questions, courses without visible material and AI drafts awaiting review. Course pulse shows student, material, assignment and review counts for each assigned course, with direct links to prepare material, create an assignment, answer questions or open the course.

AI can help draft lesson plans, outlines, quizzes, rubrics, summaries and rewrites through the existing material editor. Generated content is never published automatically; review and apply it first. When AI is unavailable, review, discussion and manual preparation remain available. Teaching Studio does not rank lecturers or claim to measure teaching quality. See [Teaching Studio](TEACHING_STUDIO.md) for details.

## 26. Marketplace, wallet, payouts and refunds

When commerce is enabled, eligible Knowledge Hub/learning resources can be purchased. AcadSyntra tracks orders/entitlements and can provide creator wallet/payout/refund workflows.

Never send money based on information outside the official AcadSyntra payment/commerce screens. Payment callbacks/webhooks are processed by the server.

If checkout times out or a balance has not updated, open Wallet → Payments awaiting confirmation or Orders → Check payment status before paying again. The server checks the payment reference, amount and currency. Repeated verification does not credit the same payment twice.

Refunds remain processing while the provider completes them. Administrators can reconcile an uncertain or failed provider outcome from the order after checking the provider dashboard; a provider reference and evidence note are required to confirm an external refund. Processing refunds reserve their amount against further refund requests.

Super Admin gateway setup: select one environment, enter its secret key, select the currencies enabled for the Paystack account, save, and use Test Connection. Blank secret fields keep existing keys. The configuration page displays the webhook URL to enter in the Paystack dashboard. New gateways remain inactive until credentials are configured. Deactivate gateways instead of deleting payment history.

## 34. Common messages and what they mean

### “Feature is under maintenance”

An administrator temporarily paused the module. Try again later.

### “Feature is unavailable/not released”

The module is disabled for normal users or a required parent feature is unavailable.

### “AI assistance is currently unavailable”

AI may be globally disabled, the specific AI feature may be disabled, or no valid provider/rule mode is available.

### “Not authorized” / 403

Your role, tenant, course membership, entitlement or policy does not permit the action.

### Session expired

Log in again. AcadSyntra converts CSRF/session-expiration failures into a login redirect with an explanatory message for the web UI.

### A file will not preview/download

The file may be unavailable, blocked by security scanning, expired-token protected or outside your authorization scope. Ask your lecturer/admin if the content should be available.

## 35. Good AI usage in AcadSyntra

- Give a clear academic question instead of random text.
- When using Grounded Companion, ask about the publication you are viewing.
- Treat AI output as assistance, not automatic academic truth.
- Check cited sources and original materials.
- Do not submit AI-generated text as your own where institutional rules prohibit it.
- Lecturers/admins should review AI grading/validation/moderation suggestions before making final decisions.

## 37. Glossary

- **Feature Management** — central Enabled/Maintenance/Disabled control for modules.
- **Tenant** — an institution/university boundary used to isolate data/settings.
- **Grounding** — limiting an AI answer to supplied authorized sources.
- **Provider** — external/local AI engine such as OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok (xAI), Azure OpenAI or Ollama.
- **Rule Engine** — deterministic non-LLM logic used explicitly in Rule-Based/Hybrid paths.
- **SIWES** — specialized industrial work-experience workspace in Research Studio.
- **Entitlement** — permission to access purchased/premium content.
- **PWA** — installable Progressive Web App version of the website.
- **Submission version** — a saved revision/history record for submitted academic work.
- **Research archive** — sealed/exportable research state managed by Research Studio.

## 32. Updated course and academic workspace experience

Students use **My Courses** as the main course workspace. Course cards summarize the course and provide direct access to the individual course page. The course page brings together materials, assignments, discussions, attendance and recent course activity. Lecturers use their separate lecturer course-management view, while the individual course and academic-tool pages use the same modern workspace design where permissions allow.

### Materials

Students see only material they are allowed to access. Lecturers and authorized administrators can open and manage material for their courses, including their own hidden/draft-managed resources where appropriate. The individual material page shows file/resource details, course context, related activity and the Material / Study Assistant when enabled.

### Assignments, discussions and attendance

Assignment pages show status, requirements, timing, attachments/rubrics and the user's current submission workflow. Discussion pages preserve search/filtering, replies, reactions, subscriptions, reporting/moderation and the Discussion Assistant when enabled. Attendance pages clearly separate student check-in/history from lecturer session creation, QR/geofence management, records and exports.

## 33. Updated Knowledge ecosystem experience

Communities, groups, learning paths, reading lists, events, challenges, leaderboard and creator profiles now use a more consistent responsive workspace design. Existing membership, visibility, moderation, enrollment/registration, voting/judging and creator privacy rules still apply.

### My Profile and Creator Profile

**My Profile** appears once in the account section of the sidebar. It contains your account identity, academic affiliation and access information. **Creator profile** is your Knowledge Hub author page, linked from publications; it is retained for everyone who shares academic work.

Open **Knowledge Hub → Creator profile** to edit your headline, position, biography, expertise, website and optional ORCID iD. The editor shows a live text preview and a link to your saved reader page. Name/photo changes use your existing account profile. Save publishes the selected details according to your visibility choices; a newly created creator profile remains private until you explicitly enable its public page.

Use **Choose what readers see** to control public access, institution, department and reputation/impact display. Account security and notification/recommendation preferences stay in their existing account settings. Public creator pages list only public, already-published work; restricted work remains in its authorised workspace. Existing publication permissions and purchase requirements still apply.

ORCID import is optional and uses the saved iD. It stores a sourced snapshot of public metadata without replacing your biography or publishing imported work. Changing the saved iD clears the old ORCID snapshot. **Academic identity review** shows up to five recent requests and reviewer feedback. Personal creator reviews are separate from department/institution adoption and do not grant organisational access.

### Publication creator workspace

If you create a Knowledge Hub publication, **Open workspace** remains available for your publication even when its current workflow state is read-only. For example, a publication under moderation can still be opened so you can see its content, status and moderator feedback. Editing and **Submit for moderation** are shown only when the current status allows those actions (such as draft, changes requested or rejected). This prevents the author workflow from incorrectly showing Access Denied simply because editing is temporarily locked.

## 34. AI provider connection messages for administrators

Super Admins managing provider configuration can use **AI Settings → Test Connection**. A failed test now distinguishes problems such as authentication, model/endpoint mismatch, TLS certificate failure, DNS failure, connection refusal, rate limiting, timeout and other network errors instead of labeling every transport failure as a timeout. The result includes a safe request identifier and diagnostic without displaying API keys. More detailed operational information is written to `storage/logs/ai-provider.log`.



## AI provider connection diagnostics (updated 2026-08-18)

When an administrator uses **AI Settings → Providers → Test Connection**, AcadSyntra now records a safe request ID and diagnostic in `storage/logs/ai-provider.log`. Network failures, TLS problems, provider authentication/model errors and rate limits are shown separately. Provider request payloads are sent as explicit JSON. If an installation still contains a Gemini 1.5/2.0 model ID that Google has already retired, AcadSyntra maps that retired ID to a supported replacement while leaving other administrator model choices unchanged.


## 38. Grok (xAI) and faster interaction behavior — 2026-08-20

Platform administrators can configure **Grok (xAI)** from **Admin → AI Settings → Providers** in the same way as the other supported providers. Configure or preserve the xAI API credential, choose an allowed Grok model, enable the provider, then use **Test Connection** before selecting it as Default Provider, Fallback Provider or a feature-specific provider.

Grok does not create a separate assistant. Existing AcadSyntra assistants continue to decide the academic feature/context first; the central AI router then selects Grok when the saved routing says to use it.

AcadSyntra now also gives faster feedback during ordinary navigation. A normal link should start loading on the first click, likely same-site pages may be prefetched conservatively, and accidental double submission of write forms is blocked. AI Fast Interactive Failover can move to the configured fallback provider sooner when an upstream provider is temporarily unreachable instead of repeatedly waiting on the same provider.

Actual AI generation time still depends on the selected external provider, model, network quality and size of the authorized academic context. The interface optimization reduces avoidable local delays; it does not fake an instant provider response.


## 39. Password creation and security request limits — 2026-08-20

### Creating or resetting a password

On registration and password-reset pages, AcadSyntra now shows the password conditions that are **actually configured by the administrator**. The list updates immediately as you type, so you can see which conditions are complete and whether the confirmation matches.

A typical policy may look like:

```text
Password requirements
✓ At least 8 characters
✓ One uppercase letter
○ One number
○ One special character
○ Password confirmation matches
```

Only rules that are genuinely enabled are shown. For example, if special characters are not required, AcadSyntra does not tell you that they are required. The current platform does not separately require a lowercase letter unless the underlying policy is extended in a future release.

### Login feedback

A normal failed login still behaves normally, but when temporary failed-attempt protection is approaching its limit, AcadSyntra can tell you how many attempts remain before lockout. When the account/email-and-IP combination is temporarily locked, the message tells you the real remaining wait time rather than displaying a generic error.

### Too many requests

Existing security-sensitive actions such as login, registration, password-reset requests, email-verification requests and two-factor challenges are protected by request limits. If a limit is reached, you should see a message such as:

```text
Too many sign-in attempts. Please try again in 45 seconds.
```

or:

```text
Too many registration attempts. Please try again in 2 minutes.
```

The countdown uses the server's actual retry time. Web pages may show a live countdown. API/mobile clients receive the same wait value as `retry_after` so they can show an equivalent message instead of a technical 429 page.

### For administrators

Use the existing **Admin → Settings → Security Settings** area. It now clearly groups:

- Password policy
- Login protection
- Security request limits
- Sessions & account security

Existing password settings control minimum length, uppercase, number and special-character requirements. Existing login protection controls failed-login attempts and lockout duration. Request-limit controls cover the currently implemented login, registration, password-reset, email-verification and two-factor request throttles.

These are runtime controls, not decorative settings: saved values are read by backend validation/rate-limiters. Platform-level unauthenticated request limits may be available only to the Super Admin because a user has no institution context before authentication.


## Email verification and browser notification controls

Super Admin can enable or disable **Email Verification Enabled** under **System Settings → Security**. It starts enabled. When disabled, users proceed to onboarding/workspace access without a verification email. Addresses remain visibly unverified until a valid verification link is used. Re-enabling the setting requires unverified users to confirm their email before protected access. Institution administrators cannot override this platform-wide rule.

For browser notifications, Super Admin opens **Notification Management**, generates delivery keys if needed using a contact email such as `mailto:support@yourdomain.com`, enables **Push Notifications** in Feature & Module Management, and enables the push delivery channel. Saved keys must be retained to preserve existing enrolled browsers.

Users open **Notification Preferences**, choose **Enable this browser**, and approve the browser prompt. **Send test notification** checks the current enrollment; **Disable this browser** removes only that device. The push preference below controls delivery to all enrolled devices. Unchecking and saving a channel or notification type now disables it.

Browser notifications require HTTPS, permission in the browser and permission in operating-system notification settings. On supported iPhone/iPad versions, install AcadSyntra on the Home Screen first. A blocked permission must be changed in browser settings; the application cannot override it. Push can work while offline/PWA caching is disabled, but an internet connection is needed to receive delivery.
